♥ WHO WE ARE

Rooted in care.
Driven by possibility.

Absolute Kids was created to strengthen families and open doors for young people through mentorship, youth development, sports, and community initiatives.

Founder Orlando Cobos believes children thrive when people invest in their potential—and when communities work together to make that support possible.

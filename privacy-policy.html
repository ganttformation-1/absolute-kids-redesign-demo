Privacy Policy

How Absolute Kids handles website visits, family-registration submissions, and your privacy choices.

Last updated November 25, 2026

1. About this notice

This notice covers the Absolute Kids website at absolute-kids-redesign-demo.vercel.app, including its family-registration form and links to voluntary external PayPal checkout. It explains the information involved in using these features. External websites and offline programs may have different practices.

2. Information you provide

  • Family registration: parent or responsible adult’s first and last name, email address, phone number, confirmation of responsibility for the children, and up to six children’s first names and ages. Gender is optional. An anti-spam field is also included in the submission.
  • Communications: information you choose to provide when emailing or calling Absolute Kids. Email links open your email application; they do not send a message automatically.
  • Privacy choices: your cookie preference categories, the time you save them, and the notice version are stored in your browser.

You do not need to register to browse. Required registration fields are marked on the form. Do not send Social Security numbers, medical records, identity documents, or other sensitive information through the registration form or general inquiries.

3. Registration and children’s information

The registration form is live. When you select “Submit Registration,” the completed fields are sent to the website’s Supabase-hosted registration backend so Absolute Kids can receive and review your request, coordinate the event, and contact you about it. A successful submission is pending review and does not guarantee a gift, enrollment, or attendance.

The form is for a parent, guardian, or responsible adult registering children. Children should not submit their own information. Provide details only for children you are authorized to register. The form does not request a child’s email address or create a child’s account.

Cookie choices are separate from registration. Neither browsing nor submitting this form grants a photo/media release, medical authorization, or participation waiver. Contact Orlando if you believe a child submitted information directly or if you want to review, correct, or request deletion of information you supplied about your child. Avoid including detailed child information in your first email.

4. Technical information and services

Hosting, registration, and security infrastructure may process IP addresses, browser/device headers, request times, requested pages, referring pages, and error information needed to deliver and protect the website and handle submissions.

  • Website hosting: this site is delivered through Vercel.
  • Registration intake: submitting the family form sends its contents to a backend hosted on a Supabase domain. This is separate from browser-only cookie preferences.
  • Google Fonts: pages request font styles and files from Google domains, exposing ordinary connection and request information to those servers.
  • Apex images: the Wes footer image and Apex sponsor artwork load from apexkeygroup.com, whose server receives the information needed to deliver them. Restored program photos, event flyers, and other sponsor logos are served locally from this website.

Infrastructure services process information needed for their functions. Contact Absolute Kids with questions about a particular registration or who can access it. Cookie settings do not block essential requests, fonts, images, or a registration you choose to submit.

5. PayPal and external websites

The home page’s donation links open PayPal checkout. Using checkout is voluntary. This website does not ask for or receive payment-card numbers through its pages. PayPal handles the payment information you enter under its own terms and privacy practices; Absolute Kids may receive transaction or receipt details through its payment account. Contact Orlando with donation or receipt questions.

Links to the existing Absolute Kids Wix website, social profiles, Arlanza PONY, sponsor sites, and other destinations take you to separate services with their own notices. Following an external link may disclose connection information to that service. There are no embedded social-media tracking widgets in the current site code.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The current website does not install optional analytics or marketing trackers. The cookie controls use the first-party absolute-kids-consent localStorage entry to remember the notice version, date, and analytics/marketing choices. This preference record contains no registration fields or payment details and is not sent to the registration backend by the consent module.

Use Accept all, Reject optional, or Customize in the banner. Reopen Cookie Settings in any footer to change your choice. Essential preference storage remains enabled; selecting an unused optional category does not install a service. New optional tracking services require updated information and a fresh choice before activation.

A saved preference is recognized for up to 180 days, unless you clear it or the notice version changes sooner. Expired or outdated records are not treated as valid consent. The record may physically remain in browser storage until you clear site data; the site asks again when it is no longer valid. If storage is blocked, your choice applies to the current page and you may be asked again. These controls apply to this site, not to separate websites you visit.

7. Retention and security

Registration submissions and direct communications are handled for event coordination and responding to you. Contact Absolute Kids to ask how long your particular records are kept or to request their removal. This notice does not promise a fixed retention period for registrations, correspondence, payment records, or infrastructure logs.

Use care when sending information online. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. If you suspect a problem involving your information, contact Orlando promptly without sending sensitive documents in the initial message.

8. Your choices and requests

You may browse without submitting a registration, leave optional gender blank, change cookie preferences, clear browser storage, and choose whether to use external checkout. Email orlando.cobos@absolutekids.org to ask about your information or request access, correction, or deletion.

Absolute Kids may need to verify your identity or authority to act for a child before disclosing or changing records. Available rights and any exceptions depend on the information, circumstances, and applicable law. For information held directly by an external service, you may also need to contact that service.

9. Updates

Changes to the website’s information practices will be reflected here with a new update date. Please review this notice when you use a new feature or submit information. Contact Orlando if a notice or form description is unclear.

THE FOUNDER'S STORY

Meet Orlando Cobos.

Orlando founded Absolute Kids through his deep commitment to family, community, and empowering young people. As a husband and father of two boys, his own experiences inspired him to help create positive spaces and opportunities for children.

His vision is to connect families, strengthen neighborhoods, and give youth the encouragement and support needed to grow into confident, resilient leaders.

At Absolute Kids, every act of care contributes to a stronger community.

✳ OUR PURPOSE

Our mission & vision.

♥

Mission

Absolute Kids is dedicated to helping children and families facing adversity through community connection, essential support, encouragement, and opportunity. The organization seeks to empower youth to pursue their goals and develop into future leaders.

✳

Vision

A future in which every child feels seen, valued, and supported to discover their full potential—growing into confident, compassionate, courageous people who positively influence their families and communities.

These are concise summaries for the redesign. Absolute Kids will approve the final mission and vision wording before launch.
